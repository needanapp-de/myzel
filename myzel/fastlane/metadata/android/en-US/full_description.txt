Myzel is an app store for free and open source software from F-Droid repositories – in the design of needanapp.de, for Android 12 and newer.

<b>Foundation</b>
• Based on the F-Droid client 2.0.3 (basic flavor); updates from F-Droid are merged
• Discover, search, install and update apps, also automatically in the background
• Installation with root or Shizuku if you like, then without asking

<b>Repositories</b>
• F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid and the needanapp.de repository are enabled
• About 40 more well-known repositories (e.g. Guardian Project, microG, NewPipe) wait under “More repositories” and can be switched on

<b>Wear OS watch</b>
• Set up the watch once via “Wireless debugging”; then Myzel installs, updates and removes apps on the watch over Bluetooth
• “Watch” category for apps with a watch version, the apps on the watch with their updates under “My apps”

<b>Favorites</b>
• Mark apps as favorites with the Myzel logo on their page
• The list of favorites opens from the menu button

<b>Backup</b>
• Save repositories, favorites, settings and appearance to a file and restore them on another device

<b>Design</b>
• Two styles: “NeedAnApp” (default) is the calm card look of needanapp.de with color worlds for light and dark; “Myzel” looks like a device, with aluminium in light mode, graphite in dark mode, orange as signal color and black displays in a dot-matrix font
• Navigation in a floating pill at the bottom; you can also swipe between Discover, Search and My apps
• The mycelium is the menu button and, on the app page, the options button
• Categories on Discover as a panel with all groups at a glance
• Appearance: light, dark or like the system, pure black, signal color, motion, UI scale and language
